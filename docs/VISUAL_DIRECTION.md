# Meadow screen

The board is the picture. Grass fills the window. When the map fits, it sits in the middle. Turn, goals, and the selected animal are paper cards on the right. Commands are a two-by-two pad under that card. Pause, speed, menu, and restart sit in a short row under the board.

The title screen uses the same grass, with wide choice bands in the center. Ink marks the keyboard choice. Orange marks the hovered choice and the action that finishes a turn.

Text is Georgia for the large words and Tahoma for the rest, drawn at the window's own resolution. Pixel art stays on whole-number scales, 32 or 64.
