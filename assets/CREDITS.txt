Animals are the top-down poses cut from the generated sheets in ast/.
The checkerboard background was removed, and each animal was saved as its own sprite.
The deer has antlers, the tiger has stripes and a tail, the wolf has pointed ears,
the buffalo has horns, and the giraffe has a long neck.

Terrain is from the free Sprout Lands basic pack by Cup Nooble.
https://cupnooble.itch.io/sprout-lands-asset-pack

Open ground is the grass. Feeding grounds are the crops. Resource nodes are mushrooms, fruit, and a bush.
The river is the water tile. Fords are tilled dirt. Rocks are the gray stone on grass.

Tiles are 16×16 masters drawn at 32, 64, or 96 pixels. Small boards are 12×10. Large boards are 20×20, and the large skirmish is 12×12.
